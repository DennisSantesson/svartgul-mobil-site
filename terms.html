1. The service

Svartgul mobil (by Allmanbiljett.se) is a free app that rewards supporters who keep their phone locked during the match. The app is published by Dennis Santesson, a private individual in Sweden ("we"). These terms apply between you and us when you use the app. You accept them when you create an account.

The app is free. There are no in-app purchases.

2. Your account

  • You create the account with your own email address and a password. Use an address you have access to.
  • The account is personal. Have only one account and do not let anyone else use it.
  • Keep your password secret. If you suspect someone else is using your account, change the password with Forgot password and contact us.
  • You must be at least 13 years old to use the app.

3. Match mode and measurement

  • You start match mode yourself when you are at the arena. The app checks once that you are there. Only matches with a verified location give points and count on the leaderboards.
  • Minutes are calculated by our server from when the phone is locked and unlocked during playing time, according to the rules in the app (Så funkar det). Figures during the match are preliminary. The final result is what the server calculates after the final whistle.
  • The measurement depends on the phone, the coverage and the times recorded during the match. We do our best but cannot promise that every minute is always counted. We may recalculate results, for example if a match time is corrected or an error is found.
  • Follow the arena's rules and stay aware of your surroundings. The app does not replace your own responsibility for your safety.

4. Points, levels and offers

  • Points and levels show how you have done in the app. Points have no monetary value. They cannot be exchanged for money, sold, transferred to anyone else or inherited.
  • No prizes or benefits are promised. If we offer something linked to levels in the future, separate terms will apply to it, and nobody is entitled to an offer just for having collected points.
  • We may change the rules for points and levels going forward, for example per season. Matches already calculated are not recalculated because of a rule change.
  • We may remove points obtained through cheating or errors.

5. Aliases and content rules

The only thing you write in the app that others can see is your alias. It is visible to other signed-in users on the leaderboard and the focus list if you have chosen to appear there.

Your alias must not:

  • be offensive, derogatory, hateful or threatening, for example towards anyone's ethnicity, religion, gender, sexual orientation, disability or age,
  • be sexual or violent, or encourage crime, self-harm or violence,
  • impersonate anyone else, for example a real person, a player, AIK, Svartgul mobil, an administrator or support,
  • contain someone else's personal data, such as a real name without consent, a phone number or an address,
  • be used for advertising or links.

We have zero tolerance for offensive aliases. The app stops many words directly, but not everything is caught. If an alias breaks the rules we may, without notice, hide it, require you to change it, remove points or close the account. An alias that has been hidden or removed cannot be chosen again.

6. Reporting and blocking

  • Report: press and hold an alias on the leaderboard or the focus list and choose Rapportera alias (report alias). The person you report never learns it was you.
  • Block: press and hold the row and choose Blockera (block). The person disappears from your lists and is not told that you blocked them. You lift a block under Profil, Integritet och data, Blockerade.
  • When three trusted users have reported an alias, it is hidden from others automatically while we review it. We review reports within 72 hours.
  • You can also email info@tickettaka.se to report something.
  • Abusing the report feature, for example coordinated false reports, is a breach of these terms.

7. Cheating and abuse

You must not:

  • trick the location check or the measurement, for example with a fake location, a modified app or automated tools,
  • create several accounts to influence the leaderboards, the stand competition or reports,
  • try to access anyone else's account or data, or disrupt the app or the server.
