Privacy policy

Svartgul mobil. Effective 1 November 2026. På svenska. If the two versions differ, the Swedish one applies.

This policy describes which data the Svartgul mobil app processes, why, how long it is kept and what your rights are. It describes what the app actually does, nothing more.

In short. The app measures when your phone is locked and unlocked during a match. Your location is checked once when you start match mode, and we only store the answer yes or no, never where you are. Other users only see your alias, and only if you have chosen to appear on the leaderboards. We do not sell data, show no ads and have no analytics or tracking in the app. You can delete your account directly in the app.

1. Who is responsible

The data controller is Dennis Santesson, a private individual in Sweden, who publishes Svartgul mobil. You can reach us at info@tickettaka.se. "We" in this policy means the publisher.

Svartgul mobil is an independent supporter initiative and is not officially affiliated with AIK. AIK receives no data about you from the app.

2. What data, why and for how long

The legal basis is given under Article 6(1) of the General Data Protection Regulation (GDPR): contract (b) means the data is needed to provide the service under the terms, consent (a) means you chose it yourself and can change your mind at any time, and legitimate interest (f) means we need the data for security and to keep the service free from abuse.

Account and sign-in

Data
Email address, password, when the account was created and when you last signed in. The password is stored only as a hash by our sign-in service (Supabase Auth). We can never see your password, and the app does not store it.
Why
So you can sign in, and to email you a 6-digit code when you choose Forgot password. The app sends no other emails.
Legal basis
Contract.
How long
Until you delete the account.

Acceptance of the terms

Data
Which version of the terms you accepted and when (server time).
Why
To be able to show which terms apply to you.
Legal basis
Legitimate interest.
How long
Until you delete the account. Only the latest accepted version is kept.

Profile

Data
Your alias, whether you want to appear on the leaderboards (yes, no or not chosen yet) and your usual stand if you have chosen one.
Why
The alias is the name others can see. The stand is preselected when you start match mode.
Legal basis
Contract. Appearing on the leaderboards is your own choice (consent), see section 4.
How long
Until you change them or delete the account.

Match mode and locking

Data
For each match you start match mode for: when you started, platform (iOS or Android), the stand you chose and whether the location check said yes or no. During the match: events when the phone is locked and unlocked, with timestamps from the phone's internal clock and its regular clock, an id for the phone's latest restart, and server times used to convert the phone's time to server time. The events include technical details about the phone's state at that moment, such as whether the app was open, screen brightness, whether a passcode is set, whether low power mode or airplane mode was on, whether notifications are enabled, and error codes from the app's measurement.
What we do not collect
What you do on your phone, which other apps you open, what you type or any content on the phone.
Why
To count your locked minutes during playing time. The server recomputes everything from the events and checks them, so the time is right even if the phone restarts or has no coverage.
Legal basis
Contract.
How long
Until you delete the account. Old matches are not removed automatically, because they make up your history and statistics.

Results, points and levels

Data
Per match: minutes, verified minutes, share of playing time, full focus, longest lock, number of interruptions and points. Also a points history and your level in the season.
Why
To show your statistics, count points and levels, and build the leaderboards and the stand competition.
Legal basis
Contract.
How long
Until you delete the account. The points history is then de-identified instead of deleted, see section 8.

Notifications

Data
If you allow notifications: the phone's push token, platform, a hash of a random key created when the app is installed, and when the token was last registered. Also which notifications were sent to you for a match. If a token is claimed by another account, the attempt is logged.
Why
To send at most four notifications per match: before kickoff, at half time, when the second half starts and at the final whistle. The key makes sure a push token cannot be tied to the wrong account.
Legal basis
Contract for the notifications themselves, which you turn on and off in the phone's settings. Legitimate interest for the key and the log of attempts.
How long
A token is removed when you sign out, when it stops working or 30 days after it was last registered. Everything about notifications is removed when you delete the account.

Reports, blocks and alias review

Data
Reports you send (which alias, reason and time) and whether your account counted as a trusted reporter (the account is at least one day old and has at least one match with a verified location). Reports against your alias. Blocks you make (the alias you saw and the time). If your alias has been hidden or needs to be changed: the alias and when it happened.
Why
To stop offensive aliases and protect users. An alias is hidden from others automatically when three trusted users have reported it. We then review it manually and either show it again or ask for a new alias. The person you report never learns who reported them, and the person you block never learns that you blocked them.
Legal basis
Legitimate interest.
How long
Blocks remain until you lift them or one of the accounts is deleted. Reports against you and the review history are removed when you delete the account. Reports you have sent remain after you delete your account, but without any link to you. A report that has been closed, for example because the alias was changed or reviewed, is deleted 12 months after it was closed.

Tester mode

Data
Only if your account is used to test the app: that the account is in tester mode, attempts to redeem a tester code (time and whether the code was accepted) and test matches you start yourself. Test matches are visible only to you and never count in leaderboards, the stands competition, points or levels.
Why
So the app can be tested without being at the arena, and to stop codes from being guessed.
Legal basis
Legitimate interest.
How long
Tester mode until you turn it off. Code attempts and test matches until you delete your account.

Technical logs

Data
When the app talks to the server, our hosting provider (Supabase) records technical data such as IP address, time, which function was called and the response code. This includes sign-ins. Our own error logs contain error codes only, never location or content.
Why
For operations, troubleshooting and protection against abuse, for example too many sign-in attempts.
Legal basis
Legitimate interest.
How long
At most 7 days.

We do not use any data for advertising, do not sell any data and make no decisions about you that have legal or similarly significant effects. Points and minutes are calculated automatically according to the rules shown in the app (Så funkar det).

3. The location check

When you start match mode, the app asks iOS for your position once (the "While Using the App" permission). The position, with its accuracy, is sent to our server, compared with the arena's area and discarded immediately. It is never stored, never logged and never written to disk, neither on the server nor on the phone. The only thing stored is the answer: yes or no. If you start without coverage, the position is kept only in the app's memory until the server has answered.

If you try again before kickoff, a new check is made the same way. The app shows the approximate distance to the arena (rounded to 100 m) when the location could not be verified. That distance is not stored either.

If you decline location access, match mode still works, but the match is not verified and gives no points. The app never uses your location in the background.

4. What other users see

  • Your email address is never shown to others.
  • If you have chosen Visa mig i topplistorna (show me on the leaderboards): your alias, verified minutes and number of matches on the season leaderboard, and your alias on the match's focus list if you had full focus. The lists are only visible to signed-in users in the app. You can turn it off at any time under Profil.
  • The stand competition only shows totals per stand, and only when at least 10 people count in the same stand and match. Nobody is named there, whether you appear on the leaderboards or not.
  • If your alias has been hidden after reports, others see it as "Dold supporter" (hidden supporter).
  • The share image is created on your phone and only goes where you choose to send it. We receive no copy.

5. Data that stays on your phone

The app stores some data locally on the phone: lock events until they have been sent, your settings, your sign-in and the random installation key (in the iOS keychain). It is used only by the app and is only sent to our server as described in section 2. When you delete the app, its files are removed. Items in the iOS keychain, such as the installation key, may remain until the phone is reset.

6. Who processes data on our behalf

  • Supabase (Supabase Inc.): database, sign-in and server functions. The project is hosted in Amazon Web Services region eu-west-1 in Ireland. Supabase is a data processor. The data processing agreement is part of Supabase's terms of service. Supabase also sends the Forgot password code with its built-in email service.
  • Expo (650 Industries, Inc., USA): relays notifications to Apple. Expo receives the push token and the notification text. Expo is a data processor under Expo's terms of service.
  • Apple: delivers notifications to your iPhone (Apple Push Notification service). Apple also processes data when you download the app from the App Store. Apple does so under its own privacy policy, and for users in the EU, Apple Distribution International Ltd in Ireland is responsible.

Otherwise data is only disclosed when the law requires it. Nobody else, AIK included, receives data from the app.

7. Transfers outside the EU

The database is in the EU. Expo and Apple are US companies, and Supabase Inc. is also a US company, so data may be processed in or accessed from the USA.

  • Supabase: the European Commission's standard contractual clauses, under Supabase's data processing agreement.
  • Expo: Expo participates in the EU-US Data Privacy Framework and refers to the European Commission's standard contractual clauses in its terms.
  • Apple: the European Commission's standard contractual clauses, under Apple's privacy policy.

8. Deleting your account

You delete your account in the app: Profil, Radera konto. The account is deleted immediately and this cannot be undone. This is what happens:

  • Deleted: email and password, the profile and alias, the terms acceptance, all matches, lock events and results, push tokens and notifications, your blocks and other users' blocks of you, reports against your alias and the review history.
  • De-identified: the points history (the totals remain without any link to you) and reports you have sent (without any link to you).
  • Unchanged: the published stand competition figures for finished matches, since they are only totals with no link to any person.

If you cannot access the app, email info@tickettaka.se from the address you registered with and we will delete the account for you.

Deleted data may remain in Supabase's backups for at most 7 days, until the backup expires.

9. Your rights

  • Access: you can get a copy of the data we hold about you. Much of it you see directly in the app under Statistik and Profil.
  • Rectification: you change your alias and stand in the app. The email address cannot be changed in the app yet. Email us and we will help.
  • Erasure: directly in the app, see section 8.
  • Data portability: you can get your data in a machine-readable format (JSON).
  • Objection: you can object to processing based on legitimate interest.
  • Withdraw consent: turn off Visa mig i topplistorna under Profil.
  • Restriction: you can ask us to restrict processing while a question is looked into.

Email info@tickettaka.se from the address you registered with and we will reply within one month at the latest.

10. Security

All traffic between the app and the server is encrypted (HTTPS). The database has access rules so that each user can only read their own data, and no function in the app discloses anyone else's email or user id. Passwords and the installation key are stored only as hashes. Only the publisher has access to the database.

11. Children

The app is for people aged 13 or older. The app does not ask for your age. If we learn that someone under 13 has an account, we delete it.

12. Changes

If the policy changes, we update the date at the top. We give notice of significant changes in good time, for example by email to the address on your account.
